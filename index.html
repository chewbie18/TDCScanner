Downloadable TUP answer sheets

Each downloaded PDF page is A4 landscape (297 × 210 mm) with two identical A5 portrait answer sheets. Cut along the center dashed line. Students handwrite their own name, year & section, date, subject and professor. Print the downloaded PDF at 100% / Actual size and keep all four black corner squares.

Important: A5 is compact. For examinations above 120 items, bubbles become small; verify your printed sheet and trial-scan before using in class.

OMR photo scanner (beta)

Use a flat, well-lit sheet and capture all four corner squares. Tap the center of each black square in order: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. This manual alignment prevents accidental grading from guessed corners. Review unclear bubbles and handwritten details before saving.

Shared live Google Sheet

Create a Google Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script, paste the included google-apps-script.gs, configure the shared secret, deploy as Web App, then paste the deployment URL here. Internet required for sync. Share the Sheet only with authorized teachers.

For classroom use, this is a basic shared-secret integration, not institutional SSO. Anyone with the deployment URL and secret could submit records. Review privacy requirements before using student information.
